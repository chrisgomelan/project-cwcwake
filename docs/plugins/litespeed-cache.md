# LiteSpeed Cache

[← Back to overview](../README.md)

Caching saves copies of pages so the site loads faster. After you change text or images, visitors (or you) might briefly still see an **old** copy until the cache is cleared or expires.

## When to use this guide

- You published a change but the **public** site still shows the previous version.
- Someone asked you to “purge cache” after an update.

## What to do (typical maintainer step)

Exact button names can vary slightly by host setup. In general:

1. In the WordPress admin, look for **LiteSpeed Cache** in the top bar or left menu.
2. Use the option your team prefers—often **Purge All** or **Purge this page**—then reload the page in a **private/incognito** window.

If you do not see LiteSpeed menus, you may not have permission; ask an Administrator or technical support.

## When to stop

If purging does **not** fix the issue, continue with [When something goes wrong](../04-when-something-goes-wrong.md) and avoid experimental toggles in advanced cache settings.
