# Rank Math SEO

[← Back to overview](../README.md)

Rank Math adds an **SEO** panel when you edit pages, posts, and many custom types (including rooms, if your setup exposes them there).

## What maintainers usually do

1. Open the page or post (or room) in the editor.
2. Find the **Rank Math** area (often a sidebar panel or below the content).
3. Fill in the **focus keyword** or title/description fields **your content strategy** calls for—follow your team’s checklist, not every possible option in the plugin.
4. Save or update the content, then view the live page.

## What to avoid without training

- Sitewide schema, redirect rules, robots settings, and import tools can affect **search results for the whole site**. Leave those to SEO leads or technical staff unless you were trained on them.
