# Orientation

[← Back to overview](README.md)

## Who these guides are for

Collaborators who **maintain** the site: update copy, publish rooms or pages, swap images, and follow agreed workflows. You do **not** need to edit code, databases, or hosting.

## Logging in

1. Open the WordPress admin URL your team uses (often `…/wp-admin/`).
2. Sign in with **your own** account. Do not share one login between several people.

If you cannot sign in, use **Lost your password?** on the login screen or ask whoever manages user accounts for a reset.

## Roles (simple version)

Your administrator decides whether you are an **Administrator**, **Editor**, or another role. In general:

- **Editor** — Can create and publish most content but should not change sensitive site-wide settings unless someone trained you to do so.
- **Administrator** — Full dashboard access. Only trusted maintainers should have this role.

If you are unsure what you are allowed to change, ask before clicking **Settings** items you do not recognize.

## What you maintain vs what you escalate

| You typically handle | You escalate to technical / agency |
|----------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Text, images, menus, room records in **Accommodations** | Blank white screens, “critical error,” site down |
| Publishing, previews, obvious typos | SSL, domain, email DNS, server errors |
| Following the plugin guides for routine tasks | Installing or deleting plugins, PHP updates, database work |
