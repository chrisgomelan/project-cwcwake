# When something goes wrong

[← Back to overview](README.md)

## Stay calm and narrow it down

1. **Note the exact page** (copy the address from the browser bar).
2. **Note what you were doing** (for example: clicked **Update** on a room).
3. **Take a screenshot** if there is an error message or a blank area.

## Safe checks you can try first

1. **Refresh** the page once (or use a private/incognito window) to rule out a stale copy on your own computer.
2. If your team uses **LiteSpeed Cache**, see [LiteSpeed Cache](plugins/litespeed-cache.md) for the approved “clear cache” step after content changes.
3. Try **logging out and in again** if buttons act frozen (rare, but easy).

## When to stop and ask technical help

Stop and contact your technical person or agency if you see any of these:

- A **critical error** message or a completely **white screen**
- **500** or **503** style errors in the browser
- The **whole site** is wrong, not just one page
- You are asked to edit **files**, **database**, **DNS**, or **hosting** panels

Send them: the URL, what you clicked, roughly what time it happened, and screenshots. Do not keep clicking “fix” options you do not understand.

## Email not sending?

See [WP Mail SMTP](plugins/wp-mail-smtp.md). If the test still fails, forward the error text to technical support.

## Room pages look blank or wrong

See [CWC Wake — Accommodations](plugins/cwc-accommodations.md) — especially the note about the active theme. When in doubt, escalate.
